"Trial immediate resuscitation" may only occur after "Perform physical examination"  takes place.

"Perform physical examination" must be the first task in the process.

Right after "Perform physical examination", in case the condition "Resuscitation need" is met, insert an extra activity "Mark: resuscitation need".
If "Mark: resuscitation need" occurs, then "Trial immediate resuscitation" must take place immediately afterwards. This is an absolute priority.

"Conduct baseline blood work" may only occur after "Perform physical examination"  takes place.

"Admit to hospital" may only occur after "Assess level of care required"  takes place.
